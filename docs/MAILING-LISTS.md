# Signing up to the mailing lists

Yes, do this, and use a new address, not your personal one.

## Why a separate address

1. These lists send a lot of mail. Your own inbox stays clean.
2. Later, software will read this mailbox for you. That means handing over a password.
   You never want that to be the password to your real email.
3. If a list is sold on or leaks, the damage is one throwaway account.
4. Everything lands in one place, so nothing depends on you remembering to forward it.

## Step 1. Make the address

1. Go to **https://gmail.com** and click **Create account**, then **For myself**.
2. Pick something plain that a real neighbour would not find odd, because real
   people will read it. Something like `leasidetracker@gmail.com` or
   `pete.leaside@gmail.com`. Avoid anything that looks automated.
3. Use your real name when it asks. You are a real Leaside resident. There is no
   reason to hide, and several associations check.
4. Write the address and password down somewhere safe.

Do not turn on two-step verification yet. When we get to reading the mailbox
automatically, you will turn it on and create a separate app password. Doing that
now just adds a step you would have to redo.

## Step 3. What to say

Most sign-up forms are one box for your email. A few will email you back and ask
who you are. Answer honestly and briefly:

> I live in Leaside and I follow planning and safety issues across the surrounding
> neighbourhoods. I would like to receive your newsletter.

Do not claim to live in a neighbourhood you do not live in. Some associations
restrict membership to residents inside their boundary, and being turned down by
North Rosedale for not living in North Rosedale is a normal, fine outcome.

## Step 4. Tell Claude when it is done

Once mail is arriving, say so. The next build reads that mailbox over IMAP and
turns every newsletter into an entry on your page, the same as the website feeds.
That work needs the address and an app password, which you will create then.

## What this unlocks

Four of the things you asked for in your very first message have no feed anywhere
and only arrive by email: the councillor's newsletter, police news releases,
Metrolinx construction notices, and the two associations next door to you that
have no website at all. This mailbox is the only route to any of them.
