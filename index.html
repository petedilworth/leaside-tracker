    • Ontario Line: Test Pit Investigations Near 20 Overlea Boulevard
      LeasideLeaside Business Park Association · 2026-11-21
      Metrolinx has advised that its Ontario Line contractor will conduct test pit investigations near 20 Overlea Boulevard on Monday, November 23, 2026, between 9:00 a.m. and 12:00 p.m. The work involves shallow, targeted excavations to confirm the location of underground utility connections as part of preparations for the future Ontario Line subway. What to Expect Temporary lane closure: The westbound curb lane on Overlea Boulevard will be closed for approximately three hours. Traffic impacts: Two-way traffic will be maintained, although minor delays may occur near the work zone. Business access: All businesses in the area will remain open. Motorists should follow posted signage for access. Pedestrian access: Temporary pedestrian detours may be required, with directional signage provided. Construction activity: Trucks and equipment may generate some noise, vibration and dust. Transit: No impacts to transit services are anticipated. Businesses, employees and motorists travelling through the area are encouraged to exercise caution and allow additional travel time. Work may be delayed or rescheduled due to weather or unforeseen circumstances. Additional Information For complete details, please refer to the official Metrolinx construction notice (PDF) . For questions or concerns, contact the Ontario Line Community Engagement Team: Phone: 416-202-5100 (24 hours a day, 7 days a week) Email: OntarioLine@metrolinx.com Website: Metrolinx Ontario Line For additional local traffic information, visit the LBPA Traffic Alerts page .
