      LeasideLeaside Business Park Association · 2026-11-25
      A free new tool to help local businesses keep employees, customers and visitors informed about traffic and construction disruptions. The Leaside Business Park Association (LBPA) is making it easier for local businesses and community organizations to share traffic and transit updates directly on their own websites. As construction continues across Leaside and surrounding areas, access routes, traffic conditions and transit services can change. Having up-to-date information helps employees, customers, visitors and delivery drivers plan their journeys. Our new free Traffic Alerts Widget brings information from the LBPA Traffic Alerts platform directly to your website. How does it work? The widget is available in three display options to suit different website layouts. Simply request your free widget, receive installation instructions and add it to your website. By sharing traffic information through more local websites, we can help keep the Leaside business community informed, connected and accessible throughout construction.
